From request to close, step by step, with what each role needs to know.

This guide follows one purchase order from the first request to the day it is closed. Each step says what happens, then lists what each role needs to know at that step. Look for your colour-coded role tag and you can skip the rest.

The words you will see on a PO

The status shown in the PO list is one word. A PO moves through these in order:

Draft → Requested → Approved → PO Issued → In Progress → Paid → Closed

Two more can happen at the side: Rejected (an approver sent it back) and Voided (cancelled by an admin, kept on file). In Progress means the PO is issued but the work is not all done and paid; Paid means the work is done and the payment status is Paid, but nobody has closed it yet.

Who does what: the roles

Roles are given to each person by an admin (Admin tab → Users). A person can hold several. Some roles can be limited to particular entities, in which case you only see and act on those entities.

Finding your way around the Purchase Orders tab

  • All POs is the full list. Use the status filter (it has Open (not closed)), the entity filter, the From/To date boxes, and the ▾ on each column heading for Excel-style filters. The search box at the top right of the page finds a PO by number or text.
  • Buttons beside it show only the work waiting for you, depending on your roles: My POs for Approval, My POs to Issue, Vendors to Approve, My Invoices to Review, My Invoices for Payment Approval, My Invoices to Pay.
  • A number badge on the Purchase Orders tab and on those buttons counts what is waiting on you.
  • Click a PO to open it. ← Back to Purchase Orders returns to the list. View Activity at the bottom of a PO shows who did what and when.
Dates, money and numbers

Dates are always yyyy/mm/dd and times are 24-hour. Money always has commas ($1,234.50). A PO number looks like PO-03-26-007: the entity's code, the year, then a running number for that entity and year.

1 The vendor has to be approved

Requisitioner asksVendor Approver decides

A request can be drafted for any vendor, but it cannot be approved, and its PO cannot be issued, until its vendor is approved. Check the vendor first so nothing stalls later.

Requisitioner
  • Pick the vendor from the Vendor list on the request form. If it is not there, press + New vendor (the form also checks for a similar vendor that already exists, and offers to use it).
  • A new vendor needs approval. You can pick it on your request straight away; the request just waits.
  • A vendor that has been deactivated by an admin cannot be used on new requests. Choose another, or ask an admin to reactivate it. Its existing POs carry on.
Vendor Approver
  • Open Vendors to Approve, or use the Approve vendor / Reject vendor buttons on the warning that appears at the top of a PO whose vendor is pending.
  • Rejecting needs a reason, which the requisitioner sees.
  • A vendor requested by someone who already holds Vendor Approval is approved automatically.
Admin

Deactivate and reactivate vendors, and manage a vendor's own logon, in Admin → Vendors. Deactivating also switches off the vendor's login.

2 Draft the purchase request Draft

Requisitioner does this stepRaising a request from a ticket does the same thing with some fields pre-filled (see the Ticketing guide)

On the Purchase Orders tab press + New Request. You can only raise requests for the entities you are assigned to; if you try another entity you will see "You can only create purchase requests for the entities you're assigned to."

The header

Entity *The legal owner (an LP, such as SunE Alfred LP) the money belongs to.

Line items

Add rows with + Add Part and + Add Service. One request can hold both. Each row has a quantity and unit cost.

  • Service line: a description of the work.
  • Part line: you must say what happens to it in inventory. The options are:
    • Spare: a part from the entity's inventory list. When it is received it is added to stock.
    • Used immediately: also a listed part, but it goes straight into a job and is never added to stock.
    • Consumable: not tracked in inventory (free text). Each unit is capped at a dollar limit an admin sets, shown on the line. A part dearer than the cap has to be a real inventory part or go in a service line.
    Spare and Used parts are searched by GCS P/N, Part ID, manufacturer P/N or description, and can only be parts on that entity's Required Inventory list. To order a part the entity does not list, an inventory editor adds it to the list first.

Saving

Press Save Draft. The required items are the Project, Entity, Vendor, Budget Category, at least one line, and a Site where the entity has sites. The ones you missed are outlined in red. A draft carries a DRAFT watermark on its printed view. A draft can be edited with Edit Draft and deleted by someone who can raise requests; only drafts can be deleted. Anything later is voided instead.

Check before you save

A part line left without a choice, a consumable over its limit, or a spare that is not on the entity's list will stop the save and be highlighted. Fix the highlighted line and save again.

3 Submit for approval Requested

Requisitioner does this step
Requisitioner
  • Open the draft and press Submit. It becomes Requested. Nothing goes to the vendor yet.
  • You can still change it with Edit Request while it is Requested (only you or an admin). This is how you fix something an approver put on hold for.
  • If you hold Purchase Rec but have no permission for that entity, Submit is disabled and says so.

4 Approval Approved

Approver does this stepRequisitioner waits, then may need to fix

Requests waiting for you are under My POs for Approval. Open one to review it. View PO shows it laid out as the finished PO, with markup, tax and total, so you can see exactly what you are signing.

Approver
  • Approve: the request becomes Approved and your name and the date go on the PO. Add your signature once under My Signature in the page header so it prints on every PO you approve.
  • Hold: parks it, with an optional reason. It is still Requested but marked on hold, so the requisitioner can fix it. Use Resume to bring it back.
  • Reject: needs a reason, which the requisitioner sees. The request becomes Rejected.
  • You can only act on requests for the entities you are approving for. You can approve a request you raised yourself.
  • Approval is blocked while the vendor is pending, rejected or deactivated, and for any part line that uses an old option that no longer exists (it asks the requisitioner to choose Spare, Used immediately or Consumable).
Requisitioner
  • Waiting on an approver is shown while it is Requested. An on-hold request says On hold; edit it and the approver can resume it.
  • If it is Rejected, read the reason, press Return to Draft, fix it and submit again. Only you (the person who submitted it) or an admin can do this.

5 Issue the PO and tell the vendor PO Issued

PO Issuer does this step

Approved requests waiting to be turned into POs are under My POs to Issue.

PO Issuer
  1. Open the approved request and press Issue PO. The page works out the next PO number (for example PO-03-26-007) and asks Assign PO number …?
  2. Press Confirm PO. The request becomes PO Issued. It is blocked if the vendor is not approved.
  3. Send it to the vendor. Print PO and Download PDF make the document. Email Vendor downloads a ready-to-send email draft (with the PDF) addressed to the vendor, with the entity in the subject. It is only offered once the PO has a number and the vendor has an email on file.
  4. The PO then shows who has told the vendor: a green Vendor contacted (with who, when, and how) or an amber Not contacted. Pressing Email Vendor sets it the first time. If you called or emailed from your own mail, press Mark vendor contacted. Clear contacted undoes it.

The printed PO has the same layout for every entity: the header lists the PO date, PO number, Requisitioner, Budget Category and Chargeable Expense, with the entity named as the company and in Bill To, then the line items, the approver's signature and the invoice email address.

Requisitioner

From here the PO is yours to track. You will confirm the parts or work in step 6, review invoices in step 8, and close it in step 11.

Vendor logon

If the vendor has a login, they see this PO (and only their own issued POs) under Purchase Orders.

6 The work is done or the parts arrive In Progress

Requisitioner does this stepVendor logon can add a service reportInventory sees the stock change

This is where the real world catches up with the paperwork. On an issued PO the requisitioner sets the status of the parts or the services. These are plain dropdowns; no evidence file is required to change them.

PO hasThe dropdownChoices
PartsParts Status (shown as PO Status if the PO is parts only)Not Ordered → Ordered → Partially Received → Received (and Pre-paid on a pre-paid PO)
ServicesService StatusNot Started → Partially Complete → Complete
Requisitioner
  • Only you, the person who raised the PO, can change these, and only while the PO is issued.
  • Add proof under Receipts & Invoices → Add Receipt: a photo or PDF of what arrived (a parts photo, or a service report for work). Accounting can add receipts too.
  • When you set Parts Status to Received and the PO has spare parts, a box asks where the units are. Choose Oshawa, Barn, Office, or the entity's on-site shelf (if the entity keeps parts on a shelf) and press Mark Received. The spare units are added to that entity's count at the place you chose. There is no default: Mark Received stays off until you choose.
  • Setting Received only adds the units the first time. Used-immediately parts, consumables and services never add to stock.
  • If some of the order arrived, use Partially Received and set Received when the last of it is in. Stock is added once, at Received, for the whole PO.
Inventory

Until the PO is received, its spare units show in yellow on Ownership and Physical Location as "on order". They are real stock only once the requisitioner marks the parts Received. Matching an invoice does not change stock. See the Inventory guide.

Vendor logon

On a services-only PO the vendor can upload their own service report as the receipt. They cannot do that for parts, which only your side can receive.

Pre-paid with spare parts

A pre-paid PO shows Parts Status Pre-paid. If it has spare parts you still have to set Received before it can be closed, because that is what adds them to stock. A pre-paid PO with only consumables, used-immediately parts or services can be closed from Pre-paid.

7 The invoice comes in

Invoice Matching does this stepAdmin can too

Open the PO and find Receipts & Invoices. It is one table with a row per receipt and invoice and the columns Receipt, Invoice, Requisitioner Review, Payment Approval, Paid. The stages of an invoice are:

Needs a receipt matched → With the requisitioner → Waiting on payment approval → Approved, ready to pay → Paid (or Sent back to accounting)

Invoice Matching
  1. Press Add Invoice (PDF) and choose the vendor's invoice. The page reads it: the invoice number and amount are filled in (you can correct them) and a checklist compares it to the PO (vendor, PO number, total or cap, currency, subtotal, tax, shipping and line items). The PO is shown beside the invoice with disagreeing figures boxed. It warns but never blocks. A scanned PDF has no text, so type those in.
  2. Same invoice twice: the same vendor cannot have the same invoice number twice (case and punctuation ignored). If it really is a separate invoice, write why in This is a separate invoice, because… (at least five characters). The same vendor and amount in the last 90 days only gives a warning.
  3. More than the PO: if the total of invoices would pass the PO total (or the cap), the box This invoice is for more than the PO is ticked for you; add a note about the extra. Flagging forces the requisitioner to void and regenerate the PO (step 8). You can clear a wrong flag while the invoice is waiting for review.
  4. Press Add Invoice (or Add Invoice & Match when added to a receipt).
  5. Match it to a receipt. In the Invoice cell choose Match to existing receipt… (or in a receipt's row Match to existing invoice…). A small ▲/▼ chip shows whether the invoiced total is over or under the PO.
  • An invoice sent back by an approver comes back to you. Fix what was wrong and press Resubmit, or delete it and upload a corrected one (Del. Invoice).
  • You can also mark a parts PO Pre-paid (a toggle in this panel) if that was missed on the request.
Requisitioner

If the parts are in, make sure you have added the receipt. Without a matched receipt the invoice waits at "Needs a receipt matched".

Pre-paid POs

Because there is no receipt, an invoice on a pre-paid PO skips matching and goes straight to the requisitioner's review.

Only issued POs

Invoice lists only include issued or closed POs. An invoice sitting on a regenerated draft waits until that draft is issued.

8 The requisitioner reviews the invoice

Requisitioner does this stepAdmin can stand in

Invoices waiting for you are under My Invoices to Review (and in the PO's own table). You are confirming that the invoice matches what you actually received.

Requisitioner
  • Approve: it moves on to payment approval. You can Undo that while the supervisor has not acted on it.
  • Hold: pause it with a reason (required) while you check something. You cannot approve a held invoice until you press Release. Held invoices do not count in the badges.
  • Send Back: returns it to accounting with a reason (required), clearing any hold.
  • If accounting flagged it "Over the PO": there is no Approve, no Hold and no Send Back. The only button is Void & regenerate PO. It copies the PO as a new draft (same entity, site, vendor, lines, category, ticket link), voids the original with the reason, moves the invoices and receipts onto the draft, and opens the draft with a banner showing the invoice amount against the draft's live total. Raise the prices until the banner shows ✓, then save and submit it again (step 3). It is only possible if the PO's parts were not received and no other invoice on it is approved or paid. The two POs are linked under Related POs. Only accounting or an admin clearing the flag frees the invoice otherwise.

The message "Only the person who raised this PO can review its invoices." means you are not that person.

9 Approval for payment (in batches)

Payment Approval does this step

Open My Invoices for Payment Approval. Each row shows its PO, vendor, invoice and amount, with a total of what is pending.

Payment Approval
  • Tick the invoices you approve and press the approve button. You can name the batch (the box suggests Approved invoice batch yyyy/mm/dd) or leave it blank. Each approval is recorded as a batch: your name, the time and the list. An invoice approved one at a time from a PO's page is a batch of one. Recent payment batches at the bottom shows how many in each have been paid.
  • Hold (reason required) pauses an invoice while it waits, or after you approved it but before it is paid. Held invoices cannot be ticked into a batch until released. Release restores it.
  • Send Back (reason required) returns it to accounting. A flagged "over the PO" invoice never reaches you; it goes back through the requisitioner.
  • You can take back an approval (Undo) while the invoice is not yet paid.

10 Pay, then confirm it here Paid

Payment does this step

The system does not send money. You pay by your own means (EFT, cheque), then record it.

Payment
  1. Open My Invoices to Pay. Filter by batch if you like (it shows "x of y paid"), and Download List (CSV) for your own records.
  2. After paying an invoice, set the date paid and optionally a reference (cheque, EFT or transaction number), then press Confirm Paid. The same box is on the PO's Receipts & Invoices table. Clear reverses it.
  3. A held invoice cannot be marked paid until the supervisor releases it.
  4. When every invoice on a PO is paid, set the PO's Payment Status dropdown to Paid. (It is also available as Unpaid or Partially Paid. It refuses Paid until every invoice is marked paid.)

11 Close the PO Closed

Requisitioner does this step

The Close PO button appears for the requisitioner only when all of these are true:

  • The PO is issued, and every part is Received and every service Complete (a pre-paid PO can stand on Pre-paid, unless it has spare parts).
  • Payment Status is Paid.
  • There is at least one invoice, every invoice is approved and paid, and every receipt is matched to an invoice (a pre-paid PO needs only the invoices approved and paid).

The labels alone are not enough: someone cannot close a PO that is still marked Unpaid in the paperwork. Press Close PO and it becomes Closed. On the Budget tab a closed PO stops counting what is "to be invoiced".

After the PO and in special cases

Voiding a PO

Admin

A PO past Draft is never deleted: an admin voids it, with a reason, and it stays on file. You cannot void a PO if its parts were already received, an invoice on it is paid or approved for payment, or it is closed. Reverse those first (an inventory adjustment for received stock, withdraw the approval, clear the payment). A voided PO keeps a read-only list of its receipts and invoices so the files can still be opened.

Related POs

Open a PO and look under Related POs. POs from the same vendor that share an invoice number or invoice file appear automatically. People with a PO role can also link two POs by hand by typing the other PO's number ("There is no PO numbered …", "A PO can't be linked to itself." and "Those two POs are already linked." are the refusals).

Linking a PO to a ticket

A PO can carry a link to a ticket so each can find the other. See the Ticketing guide. The number must look like TK-06-26-001.

Who can see history and money reports

  • PO History tab (admin, Invoice Matching, Payment Approval, Payment, Purchase Rec Approval and PO Issue): a tamper-proof record of every change, who made it and when, with filters and a CSV export.
  • Budget tab (admin only for now): each entity's budget against issued POs, approved invoices and what is still to be invoiced, by category. Only an admin edits the budget numbers.
