The Ticket System

Logging and tracking work at an entity or site, and starting a purchase request from it.

The ticket system is where every piece of operations and maintenance work at an entity or site is logged: what happened, which equipment is affected, who owns it, the vendor and quote, photos and files, and a history of every change. When the work needs money, a ticket starts the purchase request for it.

Three kinds of user

TagWhoWhat they can do
EmployeeAnyone with the Ticketing role in the inventory systemCreate, edit, comment on and delete tickets; upload and delete attachments; acknowledge assignments; link POs. View (not edit) entities, sites and equipment.
AdminAnyone with the Admin roleEverything an employee can do, plus add and edit entities, sites, locations and equipment, manage the equipment catalog and manage users.
VendorA vendor's own loginRead-only. Sees only the tickets where they are the selected vendor, with their files, history and linked PO status.

Your role comes from the inventory system and is refreshed each time you sign in. Holding only Purchase Rec (no Ticketing) does not give you ticket access. Someone who needs both needs both roles.

The same words as inventory

The ticket system uses the same words as the inventory system. An Entity is the legal owner, an LP such as SunE Alfred LP. A Site is a sub-location of an entity, which today means only Firelight Solar's rooftops. A ticket is logged against an entity or, where the entity has sites, against one of its sites. (Project in inventory is the top layer, Regular O&M or Repower; tickets do not use it.) A Location here is something different: an area inside an entity or site where equipment sits, such as an inverter house. When you create a purchase request from a ticket, the ticket's Entity and Site carry over to the PO.

The shape of a ticket

Open → In progress → Resolved → Closed (and On hold when waiting). Any employee or admin can move a ticket from any status to any other. There are no required steps and no approval.

1 Getting in

Everyone

You already have an account

Go to the ticket system's sign-in page, enter your Email and Password and press Sign in. Then you land on the ticket list. The Inventory ↗ link in the top bar opens the inventory system in a new tab.

  • The ticket system has its own login, but it checks the inventory system too. If your password does not work here but works in inventory (for example after you changed it there), signing in here picks up the inventory password.
  • Each time you sign in it re-checks your inventory account. If your inventory account has no Ticketing, Admin or Vendor role (or has been deactivated) you are refused with "Your inventory account doesn't have ticket system access…".
  • A deactivated ticket account says "This account has been deactivated. Contact an admin if you think this is a mistake."
  • There is no "forgot password" page. If you cannot sign in, ask an admin.

You are new

  1. First get the Ticketing (or Admin, or Vendor) role in the inventory system. Ask an admin.
  2. On the sign-in page press Request access and enter your email. If the inventory system recognises you, you are sent an email invite.
  3. Open the link in the email, choose a password (at least 8 characters, typed twice) and you are in.

If the email is not recognised the page says "No matching … access found for that email in the inventory system. Get access there first." Do not click an invite link while you are already signed in to another account here; sign out first.

Your account page

Click your name in the top bar. You can change your Display name (required). It replaces your email on tickets and in the activity log. Your email is read-only.

3 Creating a ticket

Employee Admin

Press New ticket (or New ticket for this entity → on an entity page, or New ticket for this site → on a site page, which does not pre-pick it). Complete the form and press Create ticket.

The ticket number. A new ticket is numbered TK-06-26-001: TK, the entity's two-digit entity code, the two-digit year, and a running number for that code and year (it restarts at 001 each year). The entity code matches the PO numbers of the same entity, so TK-06-26-001 and PO-06-26-001 are obviously related. An entity or site with no entity code, and older tickets, show as TK-00042.

If a file cannot be saved, the ticket is still created and you land on it with a message "Ticket created, but: …". Errors seen here: "Title and entity are required".

4 Working a ticket

Employee Admin work itVendor reads it

Open a ticket by clicking its row. From top to bottom the page shows:

  • Header: the ticket number, title and status pill; "Opened by … on …" and who it is assigned to; the vendor, quote #, PO #, invoice number and amount, and dates.
  • Acknowledge banner (if it is assigned to you and you have not yet): press Acknowledge. Re-assigning a ticket resets it.
  • Entity card (Entity / Site when the ticket is on a site) with its address and notes, and the Description.
  • Equipment on site: all the equipment at that entity or site, with the affected items in red. Search it, filter by location, or tick Affected only.
  • Attachments: linked purchase orders first, then files (see section 5).
  • Activity (collapsed): who did what and when.
  • Update this ticket (collapsed, employees and admins).
Employee
Admin

Update this ticket

  • One form holds: Status, Status note, Assigned to, Vendor, Vendor quote #, PO #, Invoice #, Invoice amount, Planned or unplanned (and dates), Affected equipment, and Add a note ("What changed, what you found, next steps…"). Press Save update.
  • Only fields you actually changed are logged, each as a bullet. A note on its own still creates an entry.
  • Title, Description, Entity and Site cannot be edited. If one is wrong, say so in a note, or delete the ticket and create a correct one.
  • The Status dropdown next to the title (with its Short current-status note) saves with its own Save button.
  • Delete ticket (red link) removes the ticket after a confirmation. Any employee can delete any ticket. It cannot be undone, and a purchase order that was linked is left pointing at a ticket that no longer exists.
Vendor

You see only tickets where you are the Vendor ("My tickets"). You can read everything on them, including the whole activity log and the invoice fields, and download the files. You cannot create, edit, upload, link or delete anything.

5 Files, photos and history

Attachments

  • Allowed: PDF, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC and HEIF. Maximum 15 MB per file. Anything else says "Attachments must be a PDF or a photo (JPEG, PNG, WEBP, or HEIC)" or "Attachment must be under 15MB".
  • Employee Admin add files at creation or later with Upload attachment in the Attachments section (one file at a time), and can delete them ("Delete … ? This cannot be undone.").
  • Photos (JPEG, PNG, WEBP) are shrunk to a sensible size automatically; HEIC and HEIF are stored as they are.
  • File links are valid for 24 hours. If a page has been open a long time and a link fails, refresh the page.

Activity

  • Every update becomes an entry: who, the date and time, and one bullet per change (status, status note, assignment, vendor, quote #, PO #, invoice, dates, equipment, files attached or removed, POs linked or unlinked), plus any note.
  • It is oldest first, and visible to employees, admins and the ticket's vendor.
  • It is not tamper-proof: an employee or admin can delete an entry ("Delete this activity log entry?"). Use notes to correct, rather than deleting. Creating a ticket is not a log entry; the header's "Opened by … on …" is the record. Deleting a ticket is not recorded.

6 Purchase orders and tickets

Employee Admin start and linkRequisitioner finishes the request in inventory

Start a purchase request from a ticket

  1. On the ticket, press Create Purchase Rec ↗. The inventory system opens in a new tab. You have to be signed in there, which is a separate login; the link waits for you.
  2. Inventory opens its Purchase Orders tab with a New Purchase Request already filled in: the Entity and, where the ticket is on one, the Site (taken from the ticket), the Vendor (matched by name, so it stays empty if there is no exact match), the Vendor Quote #, and a Description of TK-06-26-001: title.
  3. Nothing else is filled in. The person with the Purchase Rec role chooses the Project and Budget Category, adds the lines and saves the draft, then carries on as in the Purchase Order guide.
  4. A request made this way from an existing ticket is linked automatically, and a link shows on the ticket.
If nothing opens

Without the Purchase Rec role in inventory you land on the Purchase Orders tab with no form. On the New ticket page the button is greyed until you pick an Entity, and a request started from there is not linked (the ticket does not exist yet). Link it afterwards.

Link a purchase order that already exists

Employee
Admin
  1. In the ticket's Attachments section press Link existing PO.
  2. Type a PO number or words from its description and press Search (it shows the 15 newest if you type nothing, across all entities).
  3. Press Link on the right row. A line "Linked PO …" is added to the activity.
  4. To remove it, press Unlink beside the PO (the PO itself is not changed).

A PO already linked to another ticket cannot be taken over: "That PO is already linked to TK-… Unlink it from that ticket first." A PO already on this ticket says "Already linked".

From the inventory side

A PO in inventory has a Ticket field with Link ticket, Change and Unlink. Type the ticket number (TK-06-26-001, 06-26-001, TK-00042 or 42). A bad entry says "Enter a ticket number like TK-06-26-001 or TK-00042." The linked ticket then also shows on the ticket's page. In that PO the label may still read in the old style (TK-00042) for a newer ticket; it still opens the right one.

7 Entities, sites, equipment and users

Employee viewAdmin edit
  • Entities: the table lists each entity and site with its Entity, Site and Address. An entity or site page shows its notes, its entity code (read-only), its locations (such as inverter houses) grouped by Location type, and the equipment at each (Name, Type, Manufacturer / model, Serial #, Installed, AC kW, DC kW). Equipment with no location is under "Unassigned equipment".
  • Admin only: add or edit or delete entities, sites, locations and equipment on those pages. Add entity or site asks for a Name (required), an Entity (only when you are adding a site of an entity), an Address and Notes; the entity code is set by whoever maintains the database, not on the screen.
  • Admin Equipment catalog: the list of equipment types, manufacturers and models with AC and DC ratings, used when adding equipment. Type, manufacturer and model are required.
  • Admin Manage users: Sync now pulls approved vendors and every Admin, Ticketing and Vendor logon from inventory, creates their ticket accounts, updates roles, and deactivates people who are no longer in inventory. It never reactivates, so use Reactivate on a person by hand. You can also edit a display name, deactivate, or remove an account (a person with history can only be deactivated). You cannot deactivate or remove your own account. A vendor with no email in inventory cannot be synced.
